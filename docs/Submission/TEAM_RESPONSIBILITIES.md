# MedSphere – 3-Student Module Responsibility Plan

This plan is for viva/presentation organization. It must not be used to falsely claim authorship.

## Student 1 – Administration & Security
Authentication/login/logout; Spring Security; role authorization; BCrypt/CSRF; Admin dashboard; department management; doctor management and doctor account creation.
Viva focus: SecurityConfig, User/Role, Doctor/Department relationships, authorization.

## Student 2 – Patient & Reception
Patient registration; patient-code generation; patient search/profile/edit; medical history; appointment creation/search/status workflow; Reception dashboard.
Viva focus: PatientService, AppointmentService, validation, conflict protection, database relationships.

## Student 3 – Doctor & Clinical Workflow
Doctor dashboard; assigned appointments; consultation; prescription; consultation history/details; clinical UI.
Viva focus: ConsultationService, PrescriptionService, doctor ownership checks, completion workflow.

## Shared
Project objective/problem; architecture; JPA/Hibernate; database design; SDLC; testing; limitations; future scope.

Truthfulness rule: if asked who actually developed a part, answer truthfully.
